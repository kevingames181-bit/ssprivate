### Challenge
4. What were your biggest challenges before using SeaScope?
5. Can you tell me about a specific time when poor timing cost you money?
6. How did you make trip planning decisions before SeaScope?
7. What prompted you to look for a solution like SeaScope?

### Solution
8. How did you hear about SeaScope?
9. What made you decide to try the pilot program?
10. What were your initial concerns or skepticism?
