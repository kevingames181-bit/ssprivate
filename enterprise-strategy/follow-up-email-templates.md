## EMAIL BEST PRACTICES

### Timing
- **Post-Event**: Within 48 hours (24 hours for hot leads)
- **Follow-Up 1**: 7 days after initial email
- **Follow-Up 2**: 14 days after initial email (final)
- **Quarterly Newsletter**: Every 3 months
- **Seasonal Check-In**: 2-3 months before season starts

### Personalization
- Always reference specific conversation points
- Use their name, company, species, region
- Mention specific pain points they shared
- Calculate ROI based on their operation size

### Subject Lines
- Keep under 50 characters
- Include their company name or event name
- Be specific, not generic
- Avoid spam trigger words ("free", "guarantee", "act now")

### Email Body
- Keep under 200 words
- Use bullet points for readability
- Include clear call-to-action
- Make it easy to respond or schedule

### Attachments
- Limit to 2-3 attachments max
- Use PDFs (not Word docs)
- Keep file sizes under 2MB total
- Name files clearly: "SeaScope_ROI_Calculator.pdf"

### Links
- Use calendar scheduling tool (Calendly, etc.)
- Include demo video link
- Link to case studies on website
- Track link clicks if possible

### Signature

## TRACKING & METRICS

### Email Metrics to Track
- **Open Rate**: Target 40-50%
- **Click Rate**: Target 15-25%
- **Response Rate**: Target 20-30%
- **Demo Scheduled Rate**: Target 10-15%

### Lead Scoring
Update lead score based on email engagement:
- **Opened email**: +1 point
- **Clicked link**: +2 points
- **Replied**: +3 points
- **Scheduled demo**: +5 points

### Follow-Up Schedule
- **Hot leads (5)**: Follow up every 3-5 days until demo scheduled
- **Warm leads (4)**: Follow up every 7 days (max 3 times)
- **Lukewarm leads (3)**: Follow up every 14 days (max 2 times)
- **Cool leads (2)**: Add to quarterly newsletter only

### CRM Integration
Log all emails in CRM/spreadsheet:
- Date sent
- Template used
- Opened? (if tracking)
- Clicked? (if tracking)
- Replied?
- Next follow-up date
