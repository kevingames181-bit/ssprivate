## Design Guidelines

### Visual Style
- **Colors**: Ocean blues, professional grays, accent orange/red
- **Fonts**: Clean, modern sans-serif (Helvetica, Arial, or similar)
- **Images**: High-quality photos of Alaska fishing operations
- **Layout**: Minimal text, maximum impact

### Presentation Tips
- **Timing**: 5 minutes total (30 seconds per slide)
- **Practice**: Rehearse 10+ times before events
- **Customize**: Adjust ROI examples for audience (fishermen vs. fleet vs. government)
- **Engage**: Ask questions, don't just present
- **Demo**: Always offer to show live platform

### File Formats
- **PowerPoint**: .pptx for editing
- **PDF**: For email distribution
- **Keynote**: For Mac presentations
- **Google Slides**: For easy sharing and collaboration

### Distribution
- Email to prospects after demos
- Print handouts for events (one-page summary)
- Upload to website for download
- Share on LinkedIn for visibility
