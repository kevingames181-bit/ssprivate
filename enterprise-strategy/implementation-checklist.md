## RISK MITIGATION

### Risk 1: Low Interview Response Rate
- **Mitigation**: Multi-channel outreach (email, LinkedIn, phone)
- **Backup**: Attend local fishing events for in-person conversations
- **Target**: 60+ outreach to get 20 interviews

### Risk 2: Event Doesn't Generate Leads
- **Mitigation**: Pre-event outreach to attendees
- **Backup**: Multiple events planned (2-3 per year)
- **Target**: 20+ qualified leads per event

### Risk 3: Pilots Don't Convert
- **Mitigation**: Money-back guarantee, weekly support
- **Backup**: Adjust pricing or features based on feedback
- **Target**: 60%+ pilot conversion rate

### Risk 4: Prediction Accuracy Below Target
- **Mitigation**: Continuous model improvement, data validation
- **Backup**: Refund policy, transparent communication
- **Target**: 80%+ accuracy or refund

### Risk 5: Regulatory Issues
- **Mitigation**: Legal review of data usage, ADFG/NOAA permissions
- **Backup**: Join industry associations, advisory board guidance
- **Target**: Full compliance before scaling
