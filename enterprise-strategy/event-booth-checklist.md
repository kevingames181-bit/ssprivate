## Booth Engagement Scripts

### Opening Line (3 seconds)
"Hi! Are you involved in Alaska commercial fishing?"

**If yes:**
"Great! We help fishermen maximize profit through predictive intelligence. Can I show you a quick demo?"

**If no:**
"No worries! Do you know anyone who fishes commercially? We'd love to connect."

### Qualifying Questions (30 seconds)
1. "How many vessels do you operate?"
2. "What species do you primarily target?"
3. "How do you currently decide when to go out?"
4. "What's your biggest challenge with timing?"
5. "Are you the decision-maker for technology purchases?"

### Demo Flow (5 minutes)
1. **Hook (30 sec):** "Show me your home port" → Pull up their region
2. **Problem (1 min):** "Here's what happened last season" → Show missed opportunities
3. **Solution (2 min):** "Here's what we predict for next week" → Live forecast
4. **ROI (1 min):** "If you'd used this last season..." → Calculate savings
5. **CTA (30 sec):** "Want to try it for your next trip?"

### Objection Handling

**"How accurate is it?"**
"We achieve 80%+ accuracy on average, and we track it for every customer. Plus, we offer a money-back guarantee if you don't see ROI."

**"How much does it cost?"**
"It depends on your operation size. For individual operators, it's $2,500 per season. But we're offering a pilot program at 50% off. Based on your [X] vessels, you'd save $[Y] in fuel alone."

**"I already have my own system."**
"That's great! What are you using? [Listen] Many of our customers used [similar tools] before and found SeaScope more accurate because we use real-time Alaska data. Want to see a comparison?"

**"I need to think about it."**
"Absolutely! Let me send you some information. What's the best email? And would you be open to a 15-minute call next week to answer any questions?"

**"I don't trust technology."**
"I totally understand. That's why we offer a money-back guarantee and our advisory board includes [VETERAN FISHERMAN]. Want to talk to one of our current customers?"

### Closing
"Great talking with you, [NAME]. I'll send you [MATERIALS] today. When's a good time for a follow-up call?"

[Get contact info, schedule call, thank them]
