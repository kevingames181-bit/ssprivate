### 2.1 Platform Access

- **Data Integration**: Real-time NOAA and Alaska Department of Fish & Game data
- **Historical Analysis**: 5+ years of historical pattern data
- **Mobile & Web Access**: Platform accessible via web browser and mobile devices
- **User Accounts**: [X] user accounts for Customer's operation

### 2.2 Support Services
Provider will provide the following support during the pilot:

- **Onboarding Training**: 1-hour training session on platform features and usage
- **Weekly Check-Ins**: 15-minute calls to gather feedback and address questions
- **Direct Support**: Email and phone support during business hours (9am-5pm Alaska Time, Monday-Friday)
- **Emergency Support**: Best-effort support for critical issues during fishing operations

### 2.3 Customization

## 4. CUSTOMER OBLIGATIONS

### 4.1 Data Sharing
Customer agrees to share the following data with Provider for evaluation purposes:
- Trip dates and locations (general region, not exact coordinates)
- Catch results (species, quantity, quality)
- Fuel consumption per trip
- Feedback on prediction accuracy

**Confidentiality**: Provider will keep all Customer data confidential and use it only for:
- Improving prediction models
- Calculating ROI for Customer
- Aggregate analysis (anonymized, no identifying information)

### 4.2 Feedback Participation
Customer agrees to:
- Participate in weekly 15-minute check-in calls
- Provide honest feedback on platform features and accuracy
- Report any technical issues or bugs promptly
- Complete end-of-pilot survey

### 4.3 Case Study Participation (Optional)
If Customer achieves positive ROI, Customer may agree to participate in a case study, including:
- Testimonial quote
- ROI metrics (anonymized if preferred)
- Company name and general operation details (with approval)

**Compensation for Case Study**: [OPTION 1: Additional 25% discount on Year 2 contract] OR [OPTION 2: $500 credit]
