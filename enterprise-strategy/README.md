# SeaScope Alaska - Enterprise Strategy Materials

## Overview
This directory contains all materials needed to execute the SeaScope Alaska enterprise commercialization strategy. These deliverables support customer discovery, sales, pilot acquisition, and market positioning.

**Goal**: $50k-$150k ARR from 3-10 enterprise customers in 12 months

---

## Completed Deliverables

### 1. Customer Discovery Materials ✓

**interview-notes-template.md**
- Structured template for conducting customer discovery interviews
- Includes background questions, pain point discovery, economic validation
- Scoring system for qualifying pilot prospects

**interview-tracker.csv**
- Spreadsheet for tracking all 20 interviews
- Captures key metrics: pain severity, willingness to pay, validation score
- Status tracking and follow-up management

**target-companies.md**
- Research list of 20+ specific target contacts
- Organized by segment: fishermen, fleet operators, government/research
- Includes finding strategies and outreach priorities

**outreach-templates.md**
- 12 email templates for different scenarios
- Cold outreach, warm introductions, follow-ups, referral requests
- Includes elevator pitch, voicemail script, LinkedIn messages
- Usage guidelines and response rate targets

### 2. Sales & Marketing Materials ✓

**pitch-deck-content.md**
- Complete 10-slide pitch deck content
- Problem, solution, ROI, features, pricing, traction, team, CTA
- Speaker notes and design guidelines
- One-page handout version included

**demo-video-script.md**
- 5-minute demo video script with timing
- Opening, problem, solution, live demo, results, CTA
- Production checklist and alternative versions (2-min, 10-min)
- Customization notes for different audiences

**one-page-overview.md**
- Professional PDF handout (front and back)
- Problem, solution, features, pricing, case study, FAQ
- Print-ready format for events and distribution

**roi-calculator.html** (already existed)
- Interactive web-based ROI calculator
- Calculates fuel savings, catch improvement, total benefit
- 3-year projection and break-even analysis

**business-card-design.md**
- Complete design specifications for business cards
- Front and back layouts with QR code
- Color palette, typography, printing specs
- Cost estimates and vendor recommendations

### 3. Event & Sales Execution ✓

**event-booth-guide.md**
- Comprehensive guide for industry event booths
- Equipment list, booth design, demo setup
- Staffing roles, lead capture system, conversation scripts
- Packing checklist and budget estimates ($2,500-$5,000 per event)

**follow-up-email-templates.md**
- 12 email templates for post-event follow-up
- 48-hour follow-up, hot/warm/lukewarm leads, demo follow-up
- Pilot proposal, welcome email, referral requests
- Timing guidelines and tracking metrics

**pilot-contract-template.md**
- Complete pilot program agreement template
- Services, pricing, money-back guarantee, terms
- Customizable for different customer tiers
- Legal disclaimers and signature pages

### 4. Customer Success Materials ✓

**case-study-template.md**
- Detailed template for customer success case studies
- Challenge, solution, results, testimonial sections
- ROI calculation framework
- Interview questions and production checklist
- Design specifications for professional PDF

### 5. Implementation Management ✓

**implementation-checklist.md**
- Master checklist for all 60 tasks across 12 months
- Phase-by-phase breakdown with deadlines and owners
- Success metrics dashboard
- Budget tracker and risk mitigation
- Tools and resources recommendations

## How to Use These Materials

### Phase 1: Customer Discovery (Days 1-30)
1. Use **target-companies.md** to build your outreach list
2. Customize **outreach-templates.md** for your contacts
3. Conduct interviews using **interview-notes-template.md**
4. Track progress in **interview-tracker.csv**
5. Create materials: pitch deck, demo video, one-pager, business cards

### Phase 2: Event Attendance (Days 31-60)
1. Follow **event-booth-guide.md** to prepare for events
2. Use **pitch-deck-content.md** and **demo-video-script.md** for presentations
3. Distribute **one-page-overview.md** and business cards
4. Capture leads and schedule demos
5. Send **follow-up-email-templates.md** within 48 hours

### Phase 3: Pilot Acquisition (Days 31-90)
1. Conduct deep-dive demos with qualified prospects
2. Use **roi-calculator.html** to show personalized ROI
3. Send proposals using **pilot-contract-template.md**
4. Negotiate and sign 3-5 pilot contracts
5. Onboard customers and begin tracking success

### Phase 4: Case Study Development (Days 61-90)
1. Track pilot customer ROI and satisfaction
2. Conduct case study interviews using **case-study-template.md**
3. Write and design 2-3 professional case studies
4. Publish on website and use in sales materials

### Ongoing
- Use **implementation-checklist.md** to track all tasks
- Follow up with leads using **follow-up-email-templates.md**
- Refine materials based on market feedback
- Update case studies as more customers succeed
