# Outreach Email Templates

**Subject**: Quick question about trip planning for [SPECIES] season

```

I'm [YOUR NAME], building technology to help Alaska fishermen maximize profit per trip. I'm talking to experienced [SPECIES] fishermen to understand how you currently plan your trips.

Would you have 15 minutes for a quick call? I'm not selling anything - just learning from people who know the water.

What I'm curious about:
- How you decide when/where to fish
- What data you wish you had
- Biggest profit killers in a season

Happy to buy you coffee in [CITY] or jump on a call.

Thanks,
[YOUR NAME]
[PHONE]
[EMAIL]
```

**When to use**: First contact with individual fishermen
**Response rate**: 30-40% expected

## Usage Guidelines

### Personalization is Key
- Always research the person before reaching out
- Reference specific details (their vessel, species, location, recent news)
- Mention mutual connections when possible
- Customize the pain points to their situation

### Timing Matters
- Avoid peak fishing seasons for outreach
- Best times: Early morning (6-8am) or evening (6-8pm) Alaska time
- Avoid weekends during fishing season
- Follow up 7 days after initial contact, then 14 days

### Response Tracking
- Log all outreach in interview tracker spreadsheet
- Track response rates by template and channel
- A/B test subject lines and messaging
- Iterate based on what works

### Multi-Channel Approach
1. LinkedIn connection request
2. Wait 2-3 days for acceptance
3. Send email to work/company email
4. Follow up with LinkedIn message if no response
5. Try phone call if high-priority prospect
6. Final follow-up email after 14 days

### Success Metrics
- **Target**: 60+ outreach contacts
- **Goal**: 40% response rate (24 responses)
- **Interviews**: 20 completed
- **Timeline**: 30 days
