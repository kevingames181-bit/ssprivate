# SeaScope Alaska - Enterprise Strategy Quick Start Guide

## Get Started in 5 Minutes

This guide helps you start executing the enterprise strategy immediately. Follow these steps in order.

---

## Week 1: Setup (5 Days)

### Day 1: Review & Plan (2 hours)
1. **Read this guide** (5 minutes)
2. **Review implementation-checklist.md** (30 minutes)
3. **Set up project tracking** (30 minutes)
   - Create Trello board or Google Sheet
   - Add all tasks from checklist
   - Set deadlines for Phase 1 (Days 1-30)
4. **Block calendar time** (15 minutes)
   - Schedule 2 hours/day for customer discovery
   - Block time for material creation
   - Set aside time for interviews

### Day 2: Customer Discovery Setup (3 hours)
1. **Create interview target list** (2 hours)
   - Open target-companies.md
   - Research 20+ specific contacts
   - Find email addresses and phone numbers
   - Add to interview-tracker.csv
2. **Set up scheduling tool** (30 minutes)
   - Create Calendly account (free)
   - Set availability for interviews
   - Create booking link
3. **Prepare interview template** (30 minutes)
   - Copy interview-notes-template.md
   - Customize questions for your situation
   - Set up Google Doc or Notion for notes

### Day 3: Outreach Preparation (3 hours)
1. **Customize email templates** (1 hour)
   - Open outreach-templates.md
   - Personalize for your voice
   - Add your contact info
   - Save as Gmail templates
2. **Write elevator pitch** (30 minutes)
   - Use template from outreach-templates.md
   - Practice 10 times
   - Record yourself and refine
3. **Research first 10 targets** (1.5 hours)
   - LinkedIn profiles
   - Company websites
   - Recent news or articles
   - Find personalization points

### Day 4: Start Outreach (4 hours)
1. **Send first 10 emails** (2 hours)
   - Use fisherman outreach template
   - Personalize each email
   - Include calendar link
   - Track in interview-tracker.csv
2. **LinkedIn connections** (1 hour)
   - Send 10 connection requests
   - Use LinkedIn message template
   - Personalize each message
3. **Follow up on responses** (1 hour)
   - Schedule interviews
   - Send calendar invites
   - Prepare for first calls

### Day 5: First Interviews (4 hours)
1. **Conduct 2-3 interviews** (1.5 hours)
   - Use interview-notes-template.md
   - Take detailed notes
   - Record key quotes
2. **Document insights** (1 hour)
   - Fill out interview notes
   - Update interview-tracker.csv
   - Identify pilot prospects
3. **Send thank you emails** (30 minutes)
   - Use thank you template
   - Request referrals
   - Schedule follow-ups
4. **Send next 10 outreach emails** (1 hour)
   - Continue building pipeline
   - Mix of fishermen and fleet operators

## Common Questions

### Q: I don't have time for 20 interviews. Can I do fewer?
**A**: 20 is the target, but 15 is acceptable. Quality > quantity. Make sure you have at least 10 fishermen, 3 fleet operators, and 2 government/research.

### Q: What if I can't afford event booth materials?
**A**: Start with minimal setup: printed materials, tablet for demos, business cards. Total cost can be under $500. Focus on conversations, not fancy booth.

### Q: Should I hire someone to help?
**A**: Not yet. Do customer discovery yourself - you need to hear the pain points directly. Consider help for design/video editing if needed.

### Q: What if prediction accuracy isn't 80% yet?
**A**: Be transparent about current accuracy. Pilot program is for validation. Money-back guarantee protects customers. Focus on improvement trajectory.

### Q: How do I find event attendee lists?
**A**: Contact event organizers, check LinkedIn for past attendees, join industry associations for member directories.

## Success Tips

### Customer Discovery
- **Listen more than talk** - Let them share pain points
- **Ask for specific examples** - "Tell me about a time when..."
- **Quantify everything** - Get dollar amounts, percentages, frequencies
- **Request referrals** - "Who else should I talk to?"

### Outreach
- **Personalize every email** - Reference specific details
- **Keep it short** - Under 150 words
- **Clear call-to-action** - "Would you have 15 minutes for a call?"
- **Follow up** - 7 days after initial email

### Materials
- **Good enough > perfect** - Ship materials, iterate later
- **Use real data** - Include actual numbers from interviews
- **Show, don't tell** - Screenshots, charts, examples
- **Make it scannable** - Bullet points, short paragraphs

### Interviews
- **Be curious, not salesy** - You're learning, not pitching
- **Take detailed notes** - Capture exact quotes
- **Validate assumptions** - "Is this a problem for you?"
- **End with referrals** - "Who else should I talk to?"

## Red Flags to Watch For

### Customer Discovery
- ❌ Low response rate (<20%) → Improve email personalization
- ❌ Short interviews (<15 min) → Ask better questions
- ❌ No clear pain points → Talk to different segment
- ❌ Low willingness to pay → Adjust value proposition

### Materials
- ❌ Pitch deck too long (>10 slides) → Simplify
- ❌ Demo video too technical → Focus on benefits
- ❌ One-pager too wordy → Use more visuals
- ❌ ROI calculator confusing → Simplify inputs

### Timeline
- ❌ Behind on interviews → Increase outreach volume
- ❌ Materials taking too long → Get help or simplify
- ❌ No pilot prospects → Adjust targeting or messaging
- ❌ Losing momentum → Review goals, adjust plan

## Emergency Shortcuts

### If You're Short on Time

**Minimum Viable Phase 1** (2 weeks instead of 4):
1. **Week 1**: 10 interviews (instead of 20)
2. **Week 2**: Simple pitch deck + one-pager (skip video for now)
3. **Result**: Enough to start Phase 2 with reduced confidence

**Minimum Viable Materials**:
- Pitch deck: 5 slides (problem, solution, ROI, pricing, CTA)
- One-pager: Front side only
- Business cards: Vistaprint basic ($20)
- Demo: Live platform demo (no video)

**Minimum Viable Event**:
- No booth: Walk the floor, network, schedule demos
- Materials: Business cards + one-pager only
- Cost: <$1,000 (registration + travel)
