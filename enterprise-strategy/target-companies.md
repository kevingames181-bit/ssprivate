## Research Notes

### Finding Contacts
- **LinkedIn**: Search "[role] Alaska" with filters for location
- **Industry Associations**: Member directories (may require membership)
- **Trade Publications**: Alaska Fisherman's Journal, National Fisherman
- **Conferences**: Pacific Marine Expo, ComFish Alaska attendee lists
- **Local Chambers**: Alaska fishing port chambers of commerce
- **Harbor Masters**: Contact local harbors for fisherman referrals

### Warm Introduction Sources
- Advisory board members (once recruited)
- University connections
- Industry association contacts
- Existing network in Alaska
- LinkedIn 2nd degree connections

### Outreach Priority
1. Start with warm introductions (highest response rate)
2. LinkedIn connections with personalized messages
3. Email outreach to companies (find contacts via LinkedIn)
4. Phone calls to harbors/associations for referrals
5. Attend local fishing industry meetups if possible

### Target Metrics
- **Outreach**: 60+ contacts (3x target interviews)
- **Response Rate Goal**: 40-50%
- **Interviews Scheduled**: 25-30 (buffer for no-shows)
- **Interviews Completed**: 20 minimum
- **Timeline**: Complete all 20 within 30 days
