# SeaScope Alaska - Event Booth Setup Guide

## Overview
This guide covers everything needed to set up a professional booth at Alaska fishing industry events like Pacific Marine Expo, ComFish Alaska, and Alaska Marine Science Symposium.

**Goal**: Generate 20+ qualified leads and schedule 10+ follow-up demos per event

---

## BOOTH EQUIPMENT & MATERIALS

### Display Materials

**10x10 Pop-Up Banner (Backdrop)**
- Size: 8ft wide x 8ft tall
- Design: SeaScope logo, tagline, key benefits, contact info
- Material: Fabric (wrinkle-resistant) or vinyl
- Stand: Retractable or pop-up frame
- Cost: $200-$400
- Vendor: Vistaprint, Displays2Go, or local print shop

**Table (6ft or 8ft)**
- Usually provided by event, confirm in advance
- Bring tablecloth: Navy blue or white with logo
- Cost: $30-$50 for branded tablecloth

**Tabletop Banner Stand**
- Size: 24" x 36" or 33" x 80" retractable
- Design: "Schedule a Demo" with QR code
- Position: On table for easy visibility
- Cost: $50-$100

### Technology

**2 Large Monitors or Tablets**
- Option 1: 24" portable monitors (2x) + laptop
- Option 2: 2x iPad Pro or large Android tablets
- Purpose: Live platform demonstrations
- Setup: One for presenter, one for attendee interaction
- Cost: Use existing devices or rent ($100-$200/day)

**Laptop**
- For running demos and presentations
- Fully charged + power adapter
- Backup: Second laptop or tablet with demo loaded
- Internet: Mobile hotspot as backup (don't rely on event WiFi)

**Mobile Hotspot**
- Reliable internet for live demos
- Backup: Phone tethering
- Test before event

**Power Strip**
- 6-outlet minimum
- Bring extension cord (25ft)
- Tape down cords for safety

### Printed Materials

**Business Cards (100+ per event)**
- Front: Name, title, logo
- Back: Contact info + QR code to demo scheduler
- Quantity: Bring 150-200 (extras for high traffic)

**One-Page Product Overview (50+ copies)**
- Professional PDF printed on quality paper
- Front: Problem, solution, features, pricing
- Back: Case study, how it works, next steps
- Color printing recommended

**Case Studies (30+ copies)**
- 2-3 detailed customer success stories
- Include specific ROI numbers and testimonials
- Professional layout with photos
- Stapled or bound

**ROI Calculator Worksheets (30+ copies)**
- Printed version of ROI calculator
- Attendees can fill out during conversation
- Helps qualify leads and show value

**Pricing Sheet (30+ copies)**
- Clear pricing for all three tiers
- Pilot program details highlighted
- Money-back guarantee emphasized

**Sign-Up Sheet (Backup)**
- For collecting contact info if tech fails
- Columns: Name, Company, Email, Phone, Species/Region, Follow-up Date
- Clipboard + pen

### Giveaways & Swag

**Branded USB Drives (50+)**
- 4GB minimum capacity
- Pre-loaded with: Demo video, pitch deck PDF, case studies, ROI calculator
- Logo printed on drive
- Cost: $3-$5 each ($150-$250 total)

**Branded Pens (100+)**
- SeaScope logo + website
- Quality pens (not cheap)
- Cost: $0.50-$1 each ($50-$100 total)

**Stickers (100+)**
- SeaScope logo
- Weatherproof vinyl
- 3" x 3" size
- Cost: $50-$100

**Optional: Branded Hats or T-Shirts**
- For team members working booth
- Creates professional, unified look
- Cost: $15-$25 each

### Furniture & Comfort

**Chairs (2)**
- Usually provided by event, confirm
- For booth staff to sit during slow periods

**Small Table or Stand**
- For tablet demos
- Allows attendees to interact hands-on

**Trash Bag**
- Keep booth area clean

**Hand Sanitizer & Tissues**
- Professional courtesy

### Design Principles

**Open and Inviting**
- Don't sit behind table (stand to side)
- Keep table organized but not cluttered
- Make eye contact with passersby
- Smile and greet people

**Clear Value Proposition**
- Banner should communicate value in 3 seconds
- Use large text: "Maximize Profit Per Trip"
- Include key benefit: "80%+ Prediction Accuracy"

**Interactive Elements**
- Tablets for hands-on exploration
- QR codes for easy demo scheduling
- Live demo running on loop (attract attention)

**Professional Appearance**
- Clean, organized materials
- Branded tablecloth
- Matching team attire
- No clutter or personal items visible

## DEMO SETUP

### Platform Demo Preparation

**Pre-Load Demo Environment**
- Use realistic Alaska data (Bristol Bay, Cook Inlet, etc.)
- Pre-select popular species (salmon, halibut)
- Have multiple scenarios ready (different regions/species)
- Test all features before event

**Demo Flow (5 minutes)**
1. **Hook** (30 sec): "Let me show you how to predict peak fishing times"
2. **Dashboard** (1 min): Show map, predictions, probability scores
3. **ROI Calculator** (2 min): Walk through profit optimization
4. **Results** (1 min): Show case study and customer testimonials
5. **CTA** (30 sec): "Let's schedule a personalized demo for your operation"

**Interactive Demo Station**
- Load demo on tablet
- Let attendees explore themselves
- Provide guidance but let them drive
- Qualifying questions while they explore

### Qualifying Questions During Demo

1. "What species do you primarily target?"
2. "How many vessels do you operate?"
3. "How do you currently decide when to go out?"
4. "What's your biggest challenge with trip timing?"
5. "Would better predictions impact your bottom line?"

### Demo Backup Plan
- Download offline version of demo
- Have screenshots/video if internet fails
- Printed screenshots as last resort
- Practice demo without tech

## STAFFING & ROLES

### Booth Staff (2-3 people recommended)

**Role 1: Greeter/Qualifier**
- Engage passersby
- Ask qualifying questions
- Direct qualified leads to demo
- Hand out materials to browsers

**Role 2: Demo Presenter**
- Conduct live platform demos
- Answer technical questions
- Calculate ROI with prospects
- Schedule follow-up calls

**Role 3: Support/Backup**
- Manage sign-ups and contact collection
- Restock materials
- Provide breaks for other staff
- Handle overflow during busy times

### Staffing Schedule
- **Setup**: 2 people, 2 hours before event opens
- **Event Hours**: 2 people minimum at all times
- **Breaks**: Rotate every 2 hours
- **Teardown**: 2 people, 1 hour after event closes

### Team Attire
- Business casual (no suits, not too casual)
- Branded polo shirts or t-shirts (optional)
- Name tags with first name and "SeaScope Alaska"
- Comfortable shoes (standing all day)

## LEAD CAPTURE SYSTEM

### Digital Lead Capture (Primary)

**iPad with Form**
- Use Google Forms, Typeform, or similar
- Fields: Name, Company, Email, Phone, Species, Region, Vessel Count, Interest Level
- Submit immediately (cloud-based)
- Backup: Export to CSV at end of each day

**QR Code Sign-Up**
- Attendees scan QR code
- Fills out form on their own device
- Automatically added to CRM/spreadsheet
- Less friction, higher completion rate

### Paper Backup (Secondary)
- Clipboard with sign-up sheet
- Use if tech fails or attendee prefers paper
- Transfer to digital system same day

### Lead Qualification Scoring
Rate each lead 1-5 during conversation:
- **5 (Hot)**: Ready to buy, decision maker, qualified
- **4 (Warm)**: Interested, needs demo, qualified
- **3 (Lukewarm)**: Curious, not urgent, may qualify
- **2 (Cool)**: Browsing, not qualified, long timeline
- **1 (Cold)**: Not interested, not qualified

### Lead Notes
- Record key details during conversation
- Pain points mentioned
- Specific species/region
- Timeline to purchase
- Objections or concerns
- Referrals or connections

- [ ] Check into hotel
- [ ] Visit event venue (if possible)
- [ ] Final equipment check

**Setup (2 hours before doors open)**

- [ ] Check in at registration

- [ ] Set up banner and backdrop
- [ ] Arrange table and tablecloth
- [ ] Set up monitors/tablets
- [ ] Test internet connection
- [ ] Arrange printed materials

## CONVERSATION SCRIPTS

### Opening Line (Engaging Passersby)

**Option 1: Question Hook**
"Hey, quick question - how do you currently decide when to go out fishing?"

**Option 2: Value Hook**
"We help Alaska fishermen predict peak fishing times with 80% accuracy. Can I show you a quick demo?"

**Option 3: Pain Point Hook**
"Ever waste $10,000 on fuel because you went out at the wrong time? We solve that."

**Option 4: Curiosity Hook**
"Want to see what the fishing will look like 7 days from now?"

### Qualifying Conversation

**You**: "What species do you primarily target?"  
**Them**: [Answer]

**You**: "How many vessels do you operate?"  
**Them**: [Answer]

**You**: "How do you currently decide when to go out?"  
**Them**: [Answer - listen for pain points]

**You**: "What's your biggest challenge with trip timing?"  
**Them**: [Answer - this is key]

**You**: "If you could predict peak times with 80% accuracy, would that impact your bottom line?"  
**Them**: [Answer - gauge interest]

### Demo Transition

**You**: "Let me show you exactly how it works. This will take about 5 minutes."  
[Move to demo station]

**You**: "What region do you fish?"  
[Customize demo to their region/species]

### Objection Handling

**Objection**: "I've been fishing for 30 years, I know when to go out."  
**Response**: "Absolutely, and that experience is invaluable. SeaScope doesn't replace your expertise - it gives you data to confirm your instincts and catch opportunities you might miss. Our best customers are experienced fishermen who use it to optimize their already-good decisions."

**Objection**: "This seems expensive."  
**Response**: "I understand. Let's do the math together. If you make 20 trips per season and SeaScope helps you avoid just one bad trip, you save $10,000+ in fuel alone. That's a 4x return on a $2,500 investment. Plus, we offer a money-back guarantee."

**Objection**: "I don't trust technology."  
**Response**: "Fair enough. That's why we offer a pilot program with a money-back guarantee. Try it for one season - if it doesn't improve your bottom line, you don't pay. We're that confident it works."

**Objection**: "I need to think about it."  
**Response**: "Of course. Let me send you some information and a link to schedule a personalized demo. No pressure - just want to make sure you have what you need to make a decision."

### Closing for Follow-Up

**You**: "Based on what you've shared, I think SeaScope could save you $[X] per season. Would you be interested in a 30-minute personalized demo where we look at your specific region and species?"

**Them**: "Yes"  
**You**: "Great! Let me get your contact info and we'll schedule a time. [Collect info] I'll send you a calendar link within 24 hours."

**Them**: "Maybe"  
**You**: "No problem. Let me give you my card and some materials. Feel free to reach out when you're ready, or I'll follow up in a week or so."

## BUDGET ESTIMATE

### One-Time Costs (First Event)
- Banner and backdrop: $300-$500
- Tablecloth: $50
- Tabletop banner: $100
- Monitors/tablets: $0 (use existing) or $500-$1,000 (purchase)
- **Total One-Time**: $450-$1,650

### Per-Event Costs
- Event registration: $500-$1,500
- Travel (flights, hotel, meals): $1,000-$2,000
- Printed materials: $200-$300
- Giveaways: $200-$400
- Shipping (materials to event): $100-$200
- **Total Per-Event**: $2,000-$4,400

### Total First Event
- **Minimum**: $2,450
- **Typical**: $4,000-$5,000
- **Premium**: $6,000+

### Cost Reduction Tips
- Drive instead of fly (if possible)
- Share hotel room with team member
- Print materials locally (avoid shipping)
- Order giveaways in bulk (lower per-unit cost)
- Reuse banner and materials for multiple events
