## Feedback Scoring

### Health Score Calculation (1-10)

**Usage (0-3 points):**
- 3 points: Daily use
- 2 points: 3-5 times/week
- 1 point: 1-2 times/week
- 0 points: No use

**Satisfaction (0-3 points):**
- 3 points: 9-10/10 satisfaction
- 2 points: 7-8/10 satisfaction
- 1 point: 5-6/10 satisfaction
- 0 points: <5/10 satisfaction

**Business Impact (0-2 points):**
- 2 points: Clear, measurable impact
- 1 point: Some impact, not quantified
- 0 points: No impact yet

**Engagement (0-2 points):**
- 2 points: Proactive feedback, feature requests
- 1 point: Responsive but passive
- 0 points: Disengaged, hard to reach

**Total Health Score**: [X]/10

**Health Status:**
- 8-10: Healthy (green)
- 5-7: At risk (yellow)
- 0-4: Critical (red)

---

## Red Flags to Watch For

**Immediate Action Required:**
- [ ] Satisfaction score <5/10
- [ ] No platform usage this week
- [ ] Accuracy concerns (<70%)
- [ ] Considering cancellation
- [ ] Negative sentiment about value

**Escalation Protocol:**
- Contact within 24 hours
- Offer additional support/training
- Consider discount or extended trial
- Loop in founder for personal touch
- Document churn risk and mitigation plan

---

## Success Indicators

**Positive Signals:**
- Using platform daily
- Satisfaction 8+/10
- Specific examples of business impact
- Proactive feature requests
- Willing to provide referrals
- Interested in case study participation

**Conversion Readiness (Pilot → Full):**
- [ ] Documented ROI >3x
- [ ] Satisfaction >8/10
- [ ] Regular usage (3+ times/week)
- [ ] Positive sentiment
- [ ] Willing to commit to full contract

## Notes & Best Practices

**Do:**
- Be genuinely curious about their experience
- Ask follow-up questions for specifics
- Celebrate their wins
- Take detailed notes
- Follow through on commitments

**Don't:**
- Rush through the call
- Get defensive about criticism
- Make promises you can't keep
- Skip calls (consistency matters)
- Forget to document feedback

**Tips:**
- Schedule calls at consistent times
- Send calendar invite with Zoom link
- Have their dashboard open during call
- Reference previous feedback to show you're listening
- End on a positive note
