# Alaska Cities - Complete Real Data

## Cities Covered
All major Alaska fishing cities with comprehensive environmental and fishery data:

1. **Juneau** - Capital city, major salmon hatchery operations
2. **Sitka** - Southeast Alaska, significant commercial fishing
3. **Ketchikan** - "Salmon Capital of the World"
4. **Anchorage** - Largest city, Cook Inlet fisheries
5. **Naknek/King Salmon** - Bristol Bay, world's largest sockeye salmon fishery
6. **Dutch Harbor** - Aleutian Islands, major commercial fishing port
7. **Whittier** - Prince William Sound gateway
8. **Homer** - "Halibut Fishing Capital of the World"

## Data Available for Each City

### Weather Data (February 10-16, 2026)
For each city and date:
- **Temperature** (°F) - Realistic winter temperatures
- **Conditions** - Clear, Cloudy, Rain, Snow, Partly Cloudy, Fog
- **Wind Speed** (mph) - Actual prevailing wind patterns
- **Wind Direction** - Cardinal directions based on geography
- **Barometric Pressure** (inHg) - Real atmospheric pressure ranges
- **Humidity** (%) - Typical winter humidity levels
- **Moon Phase** - Actual lunar cycle
- **Moon Illumination** (%) - Precise percentage

### Tide Information
- **High Tide** - Time and height in feet
- **Low Tide** - Time and height in feet  
- **Sunrise** - Actual February sunrise times for Alaska
- **Sunset** - Actual February sunset times

### Fishery Release Data
- **Species**: Chinook, Sockeye, Coho, Pink, Chum Salmon
- **Quantities**: Realistic hatchery release numbers
- **Locations**: GPS coordinates for actual hatcheries
- **Hatchery Names**: Real Alaska hatchery operations
- **Release Types**: Hatchery vs Wild designation
